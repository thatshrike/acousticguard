Architecture & Privacy

Zero Audio Retention: Audio frames are held solely in a volatile circular RAM buffer. No recordings or audio files are ever written to disk or transmitted.

Two-Tier Detection: Tier 1 DSP pre-filters for vocal cord friction, respiratory rhythm, and shock transients. Only candidate frames are evaluated by Tier 2 TFLite neural models.

Offline Resilient Dispatch: If distress is validated and not canceled within the 10-second grace period, AcousticGuard dispatches via SMS, local BLE mesh relay, and an encrypted HTTPS outbox.
